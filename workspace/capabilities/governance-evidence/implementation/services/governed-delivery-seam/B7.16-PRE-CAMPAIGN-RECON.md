### 🔍 BOTTLENECK VALIDATION
**Identified Bottleneck**: Capability Ownership Consistency
- **Problem**: Out of 20 total capabilities in the EOS landscape, only 7 have complete standardized metadata (owner, status, depends_on, evidence_required). 13 capabilities are missing critical ownership metadata.
- **Impact**: 
  - Capabilities cannot be reliably discovered by products
  - No clear governance boundary for maintenance and roadmap
  - Cannot track adoption metrics across the ecosystem
  - Violates the B7.11 requirement for consistent capability ownership
- **Architectural Uncertainty**: Will the capability ecosystem fragment if ownership remains unstandardized? Can products safely consume unowned capabilities?
- **Failure Mode**: Capabilities become orphaned, products implement duplicate functionality, architectural forks emerge
