# LawyersHub Product Constitution

## Purpose
Define the operational guardrails for LawyersHub development, ensuring alignment with EOS Phase C1 principles.

## Core Principles
### 1. Product First, Platform Second
The product is the priority. Platformization only happens after evidence from real usage.

### 2. No Capabilities Stay in Product Until Evidence
No generic capabilities are created until we have:
- At least 2 products using the same pattern
- Clear duplication observed
- Measurable benefit from extraction

### 3. Vertical Slices First
Every sprint focuses on end-to-end user journeys, not individual modules.

### 4. Evidence is Gold
Every decision must be backed by evidence.
