## Purpose
Normative grammar for defining, validating, and using Evidence in Enterprise OS (Evidence First principle).
## Authority
Lead Enterprise Architect
## Scope
All phases; applies to all Evidence artifacts in Enterprise Knowledge Model.
## Normative Rules
1. Every Evidence must validate exactly one fact.
2. Every Evidence must be traceable to its source.
3. Every Evidence must have a defined lifecycle.
