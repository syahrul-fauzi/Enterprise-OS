
# ADR-0003: Evidence First
## Status
✅ Accepted

## Context
We were concerned about assumptions and guesswork driving enterprise decisions.

## Decision
All decisions, actions, and models must be supported by **verifiable evidence**.

## Rationale
- Aligns with EP-002 (Evidence Before Assumption)
- Prevents cognitive drift
- Enables auditability and accountability
- Evidence is part of Enterprise Knowledge Layer

## Consequences
- No assumptions without evidence
- All evidence is traceable
- Evidence lifecycle is managed
