
# ADR-0002: Business Capability First
## Status
✅ Accepted

## Context
We were debating whether to start with features, technology, or capabilities.

## Decision
All enterprise planning, design, and implementation starts with **Business Capabilities**.

## Rationale
- Capabilities are stable (don't change with technology)
- Aligns with "Capability First" philosophy and EP-003
- Enables traceability from vision to implementation
- Reduces scope creep

## Consequences
- No features without a corresponding business capability
- All business services implement at least one capability
- Capabilities are the backbone of the Enterprise Knowledge Model
